Buyer Commitment Reality
What you want
Sell DLC issued before buy DLC opens — zero exposure gap. Buyer has committed financially before any capital is at risk.
What you get
LOI — letter of intent only. No financial commitment, no deterrent if the buyer walks. This is the realistic outcome in this market. Do not structure a trade assuming anything stronger from a buyer who has not yet seen product.
Implication
Buy DLC opens with LOI-only cover. Full exposure from opening until sell DLC issues. A monthly or quarterly DLC structure gives a window to close the sell side before the instrument renews — quantified in the calculator below. No ICPO, no performance bond from a cold buyer.
Proof of product
Seller will certify to LBMA Good Delivery before dispatch. That cert — plus assay and bar list — is the most practical POP to present to a buyer. Confirm buyer will accept docs-only approach before committing to the buy leg.
